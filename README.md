# Dexa

**Your Android phone, one click away on your Windows desktop.**

Dexa sits in the system tray, notices your phones as they connect (USB or Wi-Fi) and mirrors them in their own windows. You get the phone's screen, sound and controls on your PC, without cables to fiddle with or commands to type.

- **Mirror with a click.** Every device you have connected before is listed in the tray.
- **Choose where things happen.** Sound on the PC or on the phone, phone screen on or off, virtual or physical keyboard.
- **Windows that behave.** Each window reopens where you left it. In free-window mode the phone rotates to match the window's shape.
- **Handy extras.** Fullscreen, game mode, screen recording and always-on-top, plus auto-reconnect when the link drops.
- **Always current.** Dexa updates itself quietly from GitHub Releases.

Built on a patched [scrcpy](https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy).

## Download

[**Dexa-win-Setup.exe**](https://github.com/b-y-t-e/Dexa/releases/latest/download/Dexa-win-Setup.exe) (Windows 10/11, x64). Enable *USB debugging* on the phone, plug it in, and click Dexa in the tray.
